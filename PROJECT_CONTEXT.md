# Street Kingz AI Ecommerce Assistant

## Project Identity

This project is NOT an AI article writer.

The goal is to build an AI-powered ecommerce assistant that helps businesses make better ecommerce decisions and automate repetitive work.

The assistant should:

- understand the business
- understand products
- understand customers
- research opportunities
- decide what work is valuable
- create useful outputs
- reduce repetitive manual work

Writing content is only one possible output.

The product is the intelligence layer, not the generated text.

---

# Founder Intent

Most AI content tools are backwards.

Typical workflow:

Keyword
↓
AI article
↓
Publish

This produces generic content.

The desired workflow:

Business goal
↓
Understand products
↓
Research opportunities
↓
Make decisions
↓
Create output
↓
Human review
↓
Publish

The system should make better decisions than a human can manually make in the same amount of time.

---

# Non-Negotiable Principles

## 1. Workflow Driven

Users should never write prompts.

The user selects an outcome.

Examples:

- Find content opportunities
- Improve existing content
- Optimise product page
- Create supporting content
- Analyse competitors
- Generate video ideas
- Improve conversions

The workflow owns the prompts.

The user owns the objective.

---

## 2. Product First

The system starts with products, not keywords.

Preferred initial input:

Product URL

Not:

Keyword.

The assistant should understand:

- product
- category
- specifications
- features
- benefits
- customer problems
- objections
- related products
- internal links
- FAQs

before deciding what content should exist.

---

## 3. Research Before Writing

The assistant should never immediately generate content.

Writing is the final stage.

Before writing, it should understand:

- what the product is
- who it is for
- search intent
- customer language
- competitors
- opportunities

---

## 4. Decisions Before Content

The assistant must decide whether content should exist.

Possible outcomes:

- Create cornerstone content
- Create supporting article
- Improve existing article
- Improve product page
- Do nothing

"Do nothing" is a valid successful outcome.

The system must not create content simply because it can.

---

## 5. Commercial First

Success is not traffic.

Success is:

- increased sales
- improved conversion
- better customer experience
- reduced manual work
- increased authority

---

# Critical Strategic Decisions

## The product is not WordPress

WordPress is the first publishing connector.

The long-term product is platform independent.

Architecture direction:

AI Ecommerce Assistant

↓

Workflow Engine

↓

Intelligence Layers

↓

Connectors

Examples:

- WooCommerce
- Shopify
- other ecommerce platforms

---

## The product is not an AI writer

The system should not compete as:

"Generate blogs faster."

That is a commodity.

The advantage is:

"Help ecommerce businesses decide what work matters."

2. Make better decisions?
3. Reduce repetitive work?
