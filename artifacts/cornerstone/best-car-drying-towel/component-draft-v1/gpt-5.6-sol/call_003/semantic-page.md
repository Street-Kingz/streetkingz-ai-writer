## What to look for in a car drying towel

### Construction and weave

### GSM in context

GSM describes fabric weight per square metre. Treat it as one specification alongside construction, dimensions, edging and wet handling—not as proof that a towel is better, safer or faster.

### Dimensions and vehicle size

Check whether the dimensions are manageable for your vehicle and drying style. A large towel may suit broad panels or a larger vehicle, but the fit decision should also account for how easy it is to control when wet.

### Edging

Inspect how the edges are finished rather than considering only the main fabric. Edge construction is a separate specification worth checking when comparing towels.

## Match the towel to your vehicle and drying routine

Think about the vehicle you normally wash, not an abstract specification sheet. Check whether you can comfortably lay the towel across the relevant panels, control it without excessive pressure and handle it once it has taken on water.

For a larger vehicle, dimensions and stated intended use deserve particular attention. For any vehicle, balance coverage against manageability: a format that looks convenient when dry may feel less practical once saturated.

Your routine matters too. If you want a straightforward wash-and-dry process, confirm that the towel's use instructions and care restrictions are realistic for you before buying.

- Match dimensions to the vehicle and the way you prefer to handle a towel.
- Consider saturated weight as well as dry specifications.
- Check the stated intended use instead of assuming every towel suits every vehicle.
- Make sure the washing and drying instructions fit your normal routine.
