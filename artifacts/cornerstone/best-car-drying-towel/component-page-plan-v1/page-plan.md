# Proposed Component Page Plan

## Topic

Choosing the best car drying towel

## Intent

commercial_investigation with informational support

## Page Direction

- Title/H1 direction: Best Car Drying Towel: A Practical Guide to Choosing the Right One
- Reader outcome: The reader should leave with a practical checklist for evaluating construction, size, handling, intended use and care, plus enough verified information to decide whether the registered product fits their vehicle and drying routine.
- Drafting authorised: **NO**
- Publication authorised: **NO**

## Proposed Component Sequence

1. **hero** (01_hero_db1ccb26)
   - Why: State the topic, reader promise and evidence-bounded angle immediately.
   - Required content: H1 direction; short supporting deck; no unsupported superlative
   - Products: none
   - Media: lifestyle_image (required_missing)
2. **quick_answer** (02_quick_answer_eb07986e)
   - Why: Answer the searcher's core decision near the top before commercial content.
   - Required content: qualified direct answer; decision depends on verified criteria
   - Products: none
   - Media: none
3. **criteria_cards** (03_criteria_cards_44e50cdc)
   - Why: Make the approved selection criteria scannable and useful without a wall of text.
   - Required content: Give an early, qualified answer explaining that the best choice depends on use case and verified criteria; do not imply that a universal winner has been established.; Explain how to evaluate a drying towel using clearly defined criteria such as microfibre construction, weave, GSM, dimensions, edging, weight when saturated and vehicle size, without asserting unsupported performance relationships.; Address microfibre directly because it is prominent in both supporting query demand and observed SERP terminology.; Provide practical drying instructions grounded in the supplied first-party guidance: use on clean, shampooed paint, remove standing water, lay the towel flat, drag gently or pat without pressure, and wring only when saturated.; Cover care instructions, including cold gentle washing with suitable detergent, avoiding fabric softener and bleach, and air drying rather than tumble drying.; Present the registered product through verified specifications and fit: 1200 GSM dual-sided microfibre, 90 × 60 cm dimensions, soft microfibre edging, suitability for cars and larger vehicles, and the limitation that it becomes heavier when saturated.; Answer the observed questions about whether drying towels work, what cloth is suitable and what towels people use, while distinguishing supported guidance from unsupported professional endorsement.
   - Products: none
   - Media: none
4. **comparison_table** (04_comparison_table_84c42ed0)

5. **rich_text_section** (05_rich_text_section_cfb857cc)
