## What to look for in a car drying towel

### Construction and weave

Construction is how the towel is made, while weave is how its fibres are arranged. Microfibre towels come in different constructions and weaves, including waffle-style options, but the label alone won’t tell you which towel is better. Check the full specification and how it suits your car instead.

### GSM in context

GSM stands for grams per square metre and tells you the weight of the fabric. That helps you understand what sort of towel you’re looking at, but it isn’t a quality score. I’d check GSM alongside the rest of the specification instead of using one impressive number to call a towel better, safer or faster.

### Dimensions

Dimensions tell you how much area the towel can cover and how easy it should be to place and control. A larger towel may be handy across broad panels, but bigger isn’t automatically better. Think about your reach, how you prefer to dry and whether the towel will still be convenient around smaller panels and tighter areas.

### Edging

The edge also touches the car, so check how it is finished rather than looking only at the main fabric.

## Match the towel to your vehicle and drying routine

Now apply those details to the car you actually wash. A larger vehicle with broad panels may make a larger towel more convenient, while a smaller car or tighter bodywork may suit something easier to position. What matters is whether you can reach, place and move it comfortably around the whole vehicle.

The real test of convenience comes once the towel has collected water. Extra weight and bulk can make a large towel harder to lift, reposition or wring. The Street Kingz towel covered next is specifically described as becoming heavier when saturated, so it’s a trade-off you should expect rather than discover halfway through the job.

Think beyond the first panel. If you’ll need to wring the towel partway through, choose something you can still control comfortably at that point. If that sounds awkward, a more manageable towel may suit you better even if it covers less area at once.

- Picture using the towel across the whole vehicle, including tighter areas.
- Allow for the extra weight and bulk after the towel has collected water.
- Choose a format you can still control comfortably partway through the job.

## How to dry a car with a microfibre towel

Start only after the paint has been properly washed and shampooed. These steps follow the instructions for the Street Kingz towel. If your towel comes with different directions, follow those instead.

- Remove standing water before starting the towel-drying stage.
- Lay the towel flat against the surface.
- Drag it gently or pat the surface without applying pressure.
- Wring it only when it becomes saturated.

## How to wash, dry and care for the towel

Wash a drying towel separately from your ordinary laundry. For the Street Kingz towel, use a cold, gentle wash with suitable detergent, then let it air dry before putting it away clean and dry.

- Do not use fabric softener.
- Do not use bleach.
- Air dry rather than tumble dry.
- Keep the clean towel away from dirt and ready for the next wash.

## Frequently asked questions about car drying towels

### What is the best cloth for drying a car?

A purpose-made microfibre drying towel is a sensible option, but I can’t honestly say every microfibre towel is better than every waffle-weave towel, chamois or other method.

### Do car drying towels really work?

They can be a practical way to remove water when the paint is clean and you use the towel as instructed. That doesn’t mean one towel will outperform every alternative.
