About this product

The Stubby Gun & Snow Foam Lance Bundle is designed to make washing your car easier, cleaner, and more controlled. The shorter gun design feels more balanced in the hand, making it easier to manoeuvre around the car without the long, awkward lance getting in the way.

Paired with the adjustable snow foam cannon, this setup delivers thick, even foam that clings to the paint and helps loosen dirt before you touch the car. The result is a safer wash and a more satisfying start to your routine.

If you’re fed up with long lances, cheap plastic fittings, or inconsistent foam, this bundle is a solid upgrade that simply works as it should.

How to use it

1) Add snow foam or shampoo to the bottle and top up with water.

2) Attach the foam cannon to the stubby gun.

3) Connect the gun to your pressure washer.

4) Adjust the top dial for foam thickness.

5) Adjust the front nozzle to set the spray pattern.

6) Apply foam evenly over the car, then rinse before contact washing.

Tech Specs

• Stubby Gun: Compact, short-handle design
• Foam Cannon: Adjustable spray pattern and dilution control
• Bottle: Heavy-duty plastic with measurement markings
• Best use: Pre-wash snow foam application
• Suitable for: Cars, SUVs, vans and bikes
• Care: Rinse through with clean water after use and store indoors

FAQ’s

Everything customers ask before buying

What adapter do I need to use this with my pressure washer?

The stubby gun uses a standard 1/4 quick-connect fitting.
You’ll need a brand-specific adapter for your pressure washer, which is not included.

Most UK users will need:

  • Karcher K-Series adapter

  • Nilfisk adapter

  • Bosch adapter

These are inexpensive and widely available online. Once fitted, the stubby gun and foam lance connect in seconds.

Is this better than a standard long lance?

Most people find the stubby gun easier to control and less awkward, especially around wheels and lower panels.

Can I use normal car shampoo in the foam lance?

Yes. Snow foam works best for pre-wash, but a pH-safe shampoo also works well though will not produce as thick foam.

Does this include spray nozzles?

Yes, five spray nozzles are included with this kid.

Is it suitable for regular washing?

Yes. It’s designed for frequent use and makes the wash stage quicker and cleaner.
