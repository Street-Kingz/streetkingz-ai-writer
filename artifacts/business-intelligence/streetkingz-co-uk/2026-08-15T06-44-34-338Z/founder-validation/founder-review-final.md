## Founder-supplied clarifications

Own-brand products are the primary focus, although this should not imply the business can never sell third-party products.

The primary geographic market is the UK.

Street Kingz primarily serves one core audience: people who clean and look after their own cars at home. They care about how their car looks, are willing to spend money looking after it, and generally value getting a better result from a better product rather than simply buying the cheapest option. Professional detailers and professional car washers are not the intended core audience.

Quality and results are more important to the positioning than low price. Value for money matters, but Street Kingz is not positioned as the cheapest option. The aim is to offer genuinely good products at sensible prices without unnecessary complexity, hype or inflated specialist-brand pricing.

The overall understanding of straightforward, premium-feeling car care for everyday owners, without the fuss or pretension associated with enthusiast/professional detailing, is accurate.
