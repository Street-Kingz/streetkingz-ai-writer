# Street Kingz organic-growth action brief

## Decision in one paragraph

The strongest bounded opportunity is a targeted on-page and internal-link test
for the Stubby Gun & Nozzle Set page. It has the clearest non-branded click
signal in the retained Search Console clusters and can be tested without
creating a new asset. Car drying has much larger visibility but weak clicks,
so it is a second, lower-confidence choice-clarity test. Contact washing has a
specific destination/link hygiene issue, but its recent click decline and
commercial outcome are not explained well enough to rank it above the first
two.

This is a HUMAN-ASSISTED ACTION ANALYSIS from retained Street Kingz evidence,
not a new Product recommendation run. No live site, database, provider, or
OpenAI operation was performed.

## 1. Prioritise: pressure-washer/pre-wash destination clarity

Target: [Stubby Gun & Nozzle Set](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/stubby-gun-and-nozzle-set/)

Problem/opportunity: the retained page title is generic (`Stubby Gun and
Nozzle Set | Premium Build Quality | Street Kingz`) while the cleaned
non-branded cluster is explicitly about stubby pressure-washer guns, nozzles,
foam lances and pre-wash. The cluster recorded 8 clicks from 217 impressions
over 365 days; the latest 90 days recorded 6 clicks from 180 impressions,
versus 2 clicks from 21 impressions in the prior 90 days.

Proposed change: keep the URL, canonical and indexability unchanged. Tighten
the opening copy, headings and FAQ around verified pressure-washer/nozzle use;
add a concise component-choice section; and add exact descriptive links to
[Snow Foam Lance](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/snow-foam-lance/) and
[Stubby Gun + Foam Lance Bundle](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/stubby-gun-bundle/).
Only catalogue-verified compatibility and product facts should be used.

Why first: it is the strongest retained non-branded click signal and is a
small, reversible test on an existing commercial destination. It does not
require inventing demand or commissioning an article.

Uncertainty and measurement: query-level position, landing-page allocation,
stock, conversion and product economics are missing. The latest increase may
be temporary and the cluster mixes gun-only and foam intent. Compare page
clicks/CTR and product conversion over a fresh comparable 90-day period, with
the component links checked separately.

## 2. Second: improve car-drying choice clarity

Target: [XL Drying Towel – 800GSM](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/xl-drying-towel-800gsm/)

Problem/opportunity: car-drying was the largest retained visibility cluster:
3,328 impressions and 2 clicks over 365 days. Latest 90 days were 1,671
impressions and 1 click, versus 1,307 impressions and 1 click in the prior
90 days. The retained site review found substantial existing copy but no
strong case for a full rewrite; the choice between the XL towel and the
Heavy Duty Drying Towel – 1200gsm is the bounded opportunity.

Proposed change: add a short, verified “which towel should I choose?” module
and a descriptive internal link to
[Heavy Duty Drying Towel – 1200gsm](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/heavy-duty-drying-towel-1200gsm/).
Use only verified dimensions, construction and intended-use facts; if those
facts cannot be verified, defer the comparison rather than fill the gap.

Why second: the visibility is materially larger than the other clusters, but
engagement is weak and may reflect rankings or shopping-heavy SERPs rather
than page-choice friction.

Uncertainty and measurement: product stock, sales, conversion, economics,
query/page positions and comparative specifications are unavailable in the
retained brief. Measure non-branded query clicks/CTR and product conversion
for a separate comparable 90-day period; stop if no verified differentiation
or commercial availability exists.

## 3. Third: fix contact-washing destination hygiene

Target: [Microfibre Wash Mitt](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/microfibre-wash-mitt/)

Problem/opportunity: the retained contact-washing cluster recorded 6 clicks
from 2,196 impressions over 365 days, but latest 90 days recorded 0 clicks
from 1,101 impressions versus 4 clicks from 743 impressions in the prior 90
days. The site evidence identified an internal shampoo link using a non-
registry path rather than the canonical
[Origin Shampoo](https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/origin-shampoo/) URL.

Proposed change: verify the non-registry URL’s redirect/canonical behavior;
then canonicalise or replace the link if necessary, and add restrained,
descriptive reciprocal links among the wash mitt, Origin Shampoo and Origin
Wash Kit. Preserve the existing substantial page content.

Why third: the correction is concrete and low-risk, but the underlying query
mix is broad and the recent decline is not attributable to the link from the
retained data alone.

Uncertainty and measurement: redirect status, landing-page distribution,
stock, product conversion and economics are missing. Measure the affected
page/query clicks and conversion after the link correction; do not expand into
new content unless the evidence improves.

## Deliberately deferred

Wheel-cleaning evidence is too small for a fourth action: 1 click from 199
impressions over 365 days and 1 click from 57 impressions in the latest 90
days. The retained query set is dominated by barrel-brush intent while one
candidate destination is the Wheel Belt Flosser. Correct mapping may be worth
a later check, but it does not justify a broad content build now.

The retained external evidence is partial and the cleaned keyword-intent
artifact reports no accepted keyword items. Rejected ideas include unrelated
or competitor/navigational terms; they are not recommendations.

## Product output versus manual analysis

The Product foundations and Slice C recommendation pipeline are available,
but no Street Kingz recommendation-generation run was executed for this brief.
The owner-reported disposable E2E result proves a bounded synthetic pipeline
scenario only; its seeded XL Drying Towel title is not Street Kingz output.
The three actions above are therefore manual, evidence-bound analysis and
must not be presented as a successful live Product run.

No public artefact contains private revenue, margin, COGS, stock quantities,
credentials, raw provider responses, or raw query dumps. Before any customer-
facing action, re-check live availability, canonical behavior, current page
state and a new measurement baseline.
