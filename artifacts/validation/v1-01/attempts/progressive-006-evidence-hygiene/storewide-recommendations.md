# Clean store-wide recommendations

Prioritise one bounded organic test around pressure-washer equipment, then address choice clarity and destination mapping in car drying, contact washing and wheel cleaning. Pre-wash ranks first because it has the strongest cleaned non-branded click signal: the 365-day baseline recorded 8 clicks from 217 impressions, while the latest 90-day trend recorded 6 clicks from 180 impressions versus 2 clicks from 21 impressions in the prior 90 days. This is search visibility and engagement evidence, not proof of commercial traction; every intervention should therefore be measured against product-level conversion and sales before wider investment.

## 1. opportunity-prewash_pressure
Customer job: Pre-wash and pressure-washer equipment
Canonical products: Stubby Gun & Nozzle Set, Snow Foam Lance, Stubby Gun + Foam Lance Bundle
Target URL: https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/stubby-gun-and-nozzle-set/
Intervention: Run a bounded on-page test for Stubby Gun & Nozzle Set. Align the title, opening copy and FAQ with cleaned phrases such as “stubby pressure washer gun” and “stubby gun nozzles”; publish only verified connection and compatibility details. Add prominent contextual links to Snow Foam Lance and Stubby Gun + Foam Lance Bundle so combined gun-and-foam intent reaches the correct products. Measure page-level Search Console clicks and CTR separately from product conversion over the next comparable 90-day window.
Why: The 365-day baseline has 8 clicks from 217 impressions, the strongest cleaned click count relative to visibility among the opportunities. Separately, the latest 90-day trend rose to 6 clicks from 180 impressions from 2 clicks and 21 impressions in the prior 90 days. The current page title and description are generic about build quality, while the cleaned queries repeatedly specify pressure-washer use, nozzles and foam-cannon combinations.
Evidence: gsc:prewash_pressure, serp:prewash_pressure, destination:prewash_pressure
Confidence: medium-low
Missing: product-level sales and conversion by landing page, stock availability, margin/COGS, verified pressure-washer connection and compatibility specifications, query-level position and CTR by destination
Could be wrong if: The 90-day increase may be temporary, and the query set mixes gun-only, snow-foam and general pre-wash intent. If compatibility is narrow, stock is constrained or the clicks do not convert, this should remain a small SEO test rather than a broader investment.
