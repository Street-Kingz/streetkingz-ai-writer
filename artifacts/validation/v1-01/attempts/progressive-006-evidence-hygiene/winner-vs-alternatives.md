# Winner vs alternatives

Winner: Stubby Gun & Nozzle Set, Snow Foam Lance, Stubby Gun + Foam Lance Bundle (Pre-wash and pressure-washer equipment internal customer-job label)

## opportunity-car_drying
Credible because: It has the largest cleaned 365-day visibility at 3,328 impressions, and latest-90-day impressions were higher than the prior 90-day window. The catalogue also has two clearly differentiated GSM variants that can support a comparison intervention.
Lost because: The baseline generated only 2 clicks, and clicks remained at 1 in each 90-day window. The current destination is already substantial and well aligned, while the SERP is crowded with product features and established retailers. There is no conversion evidence showing that the greater visibility has commercial value.
Could change if: Verified strong towel conversion, healthy margins and stock, or query-level evidence of near-page-one rankings with unusually weak CTR would move car drying above pre-wash.

## opportunity-contact_washing
Credible because: Its 365-day baseline has 2,196 impressions and 6 clicks, and the catalogue supports single-product and kit destinations. The SERP clearly reflects product-shopping and comparison intent.
Lost because: The latest 90-day window fell to 0 clicks despite 1,101 impressions, versus 4 clicks from 743 impressions previously. The destination is already well aligned, so the packet supports diagnosis and link hygiene more strongly than immediate content investment.
Could change if: Evidence that the click loss came from a correctable technical issue, combined with strong product conversion, available stock and favourable margins, would raise its priority.

## opportunity-wheel_cleaning
Credible because: The queries are product-specific and expose a correctable mismatch between barrel-brush intent and the initially selected flosser destination. The catalogue has individual brushes and a complete kit that can be linked coherently.
Lost because: The 365-day baseline is only 199 impressions and 1 click, and the latest 90-day window has just 57 impressions. That scale is materially weaker than the pre-wash signal and does not justify broad investment without sales evidence.
Could change if: Higher page-level visibility for The XL Barrel Brush, demonstrated conversion for wheel products, or evidence that destination remapping unlocks a larger relevant query set would improve its ranking.
