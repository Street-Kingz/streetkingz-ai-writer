# DIY plan — improve the car-drying destination

## Objective
Improve the existing Heavy Duty Drying Towel destination so it answers the observed car-drying choice intent and routes users to relevant wash/dry products.

## Target URL and cluster
https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/heavy-duty-drying-towel-1200gsm/ — car_drying / commercial investigation.

## Gap
The available SERP evidence shows recurring questions about waffle weave, professional drying practice and towel choice, while the current evidence does not establish that a new article is required.

## Prerequisites and access
Validated WordPress content-editor access, current page backup, Product Facts and a read-only Search Console check when available. Skills: basic ecommerce SEO, careful product copy editing and link QA. Difficulty: moderate. Effort: one editing session plus QA and later review.

## Exact changes
1. Preserve URL, canonical, price, availability and validated specifications.
2. Rewrite the first screen to state the drying use case and who should consider this format.
3. Add a short “choose by use case” section contrasting coverage/handling and towel formats without universal GSM or construction claims.
4. Add supported product facts for size, construction and intended use; attribute any manufacturer positioning.
5. Add a concise comparison to the existing XL drying towel where the evidence supports size/handling distinction; do not declare a universal winner.
6. Link to the validated wash/dry kit and relevant wash page using descriptive, non-spammy anchors such as “wash and dry kit” and “car drying towel”.
7. Keep the page product-led; do not create a generic article unless later evidence proves the product page cannot satisfy the cluster.

## Content headings
- Who this drying towel is for
- How to compare drying-towel formats
- Size, handling and use-case considerations
- Related wash and drying options

## Avoid
No invented reviews, rankings, competitor claims, guaranteed outcomes, unsupported GSM causation, stock/revenue claims or new URLs.

## Ordered QA
Preview on mobile and desktop; verify one clear title; check Product Facts; verify canonical/indexability unchanged; test every internal link; check no unsupported superiority or article-by-default drift; record the final content hash.

## Verification and monitoring
When Search Console transport is available, capture the exact query/page window. Reassess after one complete comparable period; do not interpret missing data as zero. Stop if fresh evidence shows a different destination, supply constraint, or no meaningful demand.
