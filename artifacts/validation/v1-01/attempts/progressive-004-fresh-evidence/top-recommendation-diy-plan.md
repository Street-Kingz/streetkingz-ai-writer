# DIY plan — improve the existing car-drying destination

## Objective
Make the existing XL Drying Towel product page the clearest destination for fresh car-drying choice intent, while preserving the URL and avoiding unsupported technical superiority claims.

## Target and affected products
Target: https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/xl-drying-towel-800gsm/

Also support the shared drying job through:
- https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/heavy-duty-drying-towel-1200gsm/
- https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/twisted-loop-power-pack/
- https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/the-origin-ultra-wash-dry-kit/
- https://streetkingz.co.uk/product/xl-wash-dry-set/

## Intent and gap
Intent: commercial investigation — choosing a car drying towel. Fresh SERPs contain product/category results and People Also Ask coverage. The page should explain use-case differences and route buyers to validated options without asserting a universal GSM-to-performance rule.

## Prerequisites, skills and effort
Requires read/write WordPress approval, a current page backup, current Product Facts and Search Console read access. Skills: basic ecommerce editing, internal linking and QA. Difficulty: moderate. Effort: one focused editing session plus preview and link checks.

## Exact changes
1. Preserve the XL Drying Towel URL, canonical, product identity, validated facts, price and availability fields.
2. Rewrite the opening to state who the towel is for and that “best” depends on coverage, handling and use case.
3. Add a section titled “Choosing between drying-towel formats” covering size, format and handling as practical considerations; present GSM as a specification, not a universal performance guarantee.
4. Add a short comparison block linking to the Heavy Duty towel and Twisted Loop Power Pack with descriptive anchors such as “Heavy Duty Drying Towel” and “Twisted Loop Power Pack”.
5. Add a related wash/dry path linking to Origin Ultra Wash & Dry Kit and XL Wash & Dry Set with anchors “wash and dry kit” and “wash and dry set”.
6. Keep the page product-led; do not create a new article unless later evidence proves the product page cannot satisfy the cluster.

## Supported facts and claims to avoid
Use only validated Product Facts for named products. Do not claim universal absorbency, GSM causation, guaranteed drying speed, superiority, reviews, sales, stock or conversion outcomes. Do not infer category-wide performance from one product’s specifications.

## QA and verification
Preview mobile and desktop; confirm one clear title; verify every linked URL; confirm canonical and indexability remain unchanged; check that no unsupported technical claim or competitor assertion was introduced; record the final content hash. Reassess after one complete Search Console period using query/page data and do not treat missing data as zero.

## Stop conditions
Stop if the live page is not indexable, a linked product URL changes, Product Facts conflict with the page, stock/supply constraints make additional demand undesirable, or fresh evidence changes the winning cluster.
