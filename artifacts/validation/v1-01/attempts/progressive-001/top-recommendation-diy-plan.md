# DIY Plan — Improve the Existing Drying-Towel Product Page

## Objective
Make the existing drying-towel page answer the observed choice questions clearly and route visitors to the appropriate existing product path without making unsupported performance claims.

## Prerequisites
Validated CMS access, a current page backup/export, product facts, and a read-only Search Console check when available.

## Required access and skills
WordPress content editing access; basic ecommerce SEO and copy-editing skills; no paid Product access required.

## Difficulty and effort
Moderate difficulty; one focused editing session plus QA and later observation.

## Ordered steps
1. Record the current URL, title, canonical and existing internal links.
2. Preserve the current product facts and remove no validated specifications.
3. Rewrite the opening so the use case and relevant choice are clear before specifications.
4. Explain the available towel options as market terminology, not universal performance rules.
5. Add a concise size/handling/use-case comparison using only supported facts.
6. Link contextually to the relevant wash/dry kit and adjacent validated products.
7. Keep price, availability and product claims aligned with the current store record.
8. Do not add invented reviews, rankings, competitor claims, guarantees or causal GSM claims.

## What not to change
Do not change the URL, product identity, canonical, price or availability without an independent approved reason. Do not add a new article merely because the SERP contains questions.

## Warnings
Search Console, sales, stock and margin evidence were unavailable. Do not claim increased traffic, revenue or conversion as an outcome.

## QA checklist
- One clear page purpose and title.
- Product facts match the authoritative record.
- No unsupported superiority or universal technical claim.
- Links resolve to validated existing URLs.
- Mobile and desktop rendering remain readable.
- Canonical and indexability are unchanged unless separately approved.

## Verification and monitoring
Re-read the page after publication in the CMS preview, verify canonical/indexability, then capture Search Console query/page data when transport is available. Reassess after a complete comparable period; do not treat missing data as zero.
