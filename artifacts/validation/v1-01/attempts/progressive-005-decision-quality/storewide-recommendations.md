# Store-wide recommendations

Allocate effort first to clusters with relevant non-branded Search Console traction and clear product-led SERPs. Prioritise intent routing, on-page relevance and internal linking over new content: pre-wash has the strongest near-page-one commercial traction; drying has the largest demonstrated visibility gap; contact washing and wheel cleaning have narrower but actionable query footprints. Defer clusters whose keyword evidence is off-intent or whose first-party visibility is negligible.

## 1. opportunity-prewash_pressure
Intervention: Consolidate pre-wash intent across the three existing destinations. Make the Stubby Gun + Foam Lance Bundle the primary landing page for combination queries such as stubby gun with foam cannon or snow foam lance, while retaining the individual gun and lance pages for component-specific searches. Strengthen descriptive internal links among these pages and expose verified bundle contents, compatibility and setup information in titles, headings, specifications and FAQs.
Why: This cluster has the clearest commercial traction: the bundle received five non-branded clicks from 81 impressions around position 10 for an exact combination query, while related bundle and category terms also sit near page one. The SERP is strongly product-led and includes popular-product features, making destination alignment more actionable than publishing new material. The supplied keyword ideas are largely off-topic, so first-party query evidence carries more weight.
Evidence: serp:prewash_pressure, gsc:prewash_pressure, destination:prewash_pressure, keyword:prewash_pressure
Confidence: medium
Missing: product-level sales, stock movement, margin/COGS, conversion, refunds
Could be wrong if: The bundle may have weak margin, limited stock or lower conversion than the component products. Unverified pressure-washer compatibility could also prevent meaningful page expansion.

## 2. opportunity-car_drying
Intervention: Resolve broad-versus-specific drying intent across the existing pages. Preserve the Heavy Duty 1200GSM page's foothold for broad car-drying-towel searches, keep the XL 800GSM page focused on XL and 800GSM searches, and use the drying category and bundle pages to compare use cases rather than duplicate product targeting. Correct the malformed 1200GSM title ending, add a factual 800GSM-versus-1200GSM comparison, and strengthen descriptive links among the two towels and relevant drying bundles.
Why: The cluster has substantial demonstrated visibility but almost no non-branded click capture. The 1200GSM page recorded 1,043 impressions at about position 29 for the exact broad query over 365 days, while the XL page already appears around positions 7–13 for several XL-specific terms. The SERP supports both product and collection destinations and includes comparison questions, so clearer intent ownership and comparison support address an observed gap without requiring a new article.
Evidence: serp:car_drying, gsc:car_drying, destination:car_drying, keyword:car_drying
Confidence: medium-low
Missing: reliable external keyword demand, product-level sales, stock movement, margin/COGS, conversion, refunds
Could be wrong if: The high impression count may not translate into qualified demand, and the lack of relevant keyword-ideas data reduces market-size confidence. Commercial performance could also favour a different towel or bundle.

## 3. opportunity-contact_washing
Intervention: Clarify intent between the existing wash-kit guide, the Microfibre Wash Mitt page and wash-kit product pages. Keep the guide centred on complete setup and kit searches, retain its useful mitt section, and add prominent contextual links to the mitt and appropriate kits. Focus the mitt product page on product searches and verified care guidance, including how to wash the mitt and the mitt-versus-sponge questions visible in search results. Remove or consolidate parameterised add-to-cart URLs from internal search pathways where controllable.
Why: The existing guide has 515 impressions for best car wash kit but no clicks and an average position around 35, while the product page receives recurring impressions for microfibre wash-mitt variants and care-related searches. The SERP mixes products, collections and comparison content, so separating destination roles is more defensible than creating another competing page. This ranks below drying because most product-query positions remain outside page one.
Evidence: keyword:contact_washing, serp:contact_washing, gsc:contact_washing, destination:contact_washing
Confidence: medium
Missing: product-level sales, stock movement, margin/COGS, conversion, refunds
Could be wrong if: The guide may already be the stronger destination for some mitt-comparison queries, so excessive narrowing could lose existing rankings. High SERP competition may also limit gains from on-site changes alone.

## 4. opportunity-wheel_cleaning
Intervention: Protect the Wheel Belt Flosser's existing long-tail visibility while expanding the XL Barrel Brush around exact barrel-cleaning intent. Align the XL page title and primary heading more directly with wheel barrel brush terminology, preserve the Slim Barrel Brush for tight-gap searches, and connect all three tools through the Wheel Cleaning Kit using descriptive links and a factual tool-selection comparison.
Why: The flosser already ranks around positions 3–4 for relevant non-branded terms, and the XL Barrel Brush earned a click for wheel barrel brush while its latest window improved to roughly position 12. The SERP supports individual products, collections and comparisons, and the catalogue has distinct tools for those needs. It ranks below contact washing because the observed query footprint is smaller and much of the supplied keyword-ideas demand concerns paint, repair or protection rather than cleaning brushes.
Evidence: keyword:wheel_cleaning, serp:wheel_cleaning, gsc:wheel_cleaning, destination:wheel_cleaning
Confidence: medium
Missing: product-level sales, stock movement, margin/COGS, conversion, refunds
Could be wrong if: The long-tail rankings may represent very limited demand, and changing already relevant flosser targeting could reduce its current visibility. Commercial constraints could also make the kit or another brush a better destination.
