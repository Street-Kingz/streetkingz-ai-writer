# Winner vs alternatives

Winner: opportunity-prewash_pressure

## opportunity-glass_cleaning
Credible because: The car-glass-cleaner SERP is transactional and product-led, with popular products, buying comparisons and clear questions about haze and streak-free cleaning. The catalogue also covers cleaner, cloth and kit destinations.
Lost because: Search Console contains only 13 cluster rows with almost no meaningful non-branded visibility, while the keyword ideas mostly concern repair, polish, replacement or water repellents rather than the supplied cleaner and cloth.
Could change if: Relevant car-glass-cleaner demand data, meaningful impressions for the cleaner or clarity kit, or strong conversion and margin evidence could move this cluster ahead of wheel cleaning.

## opportunity-interior_cleaning
Credible because: The SERP supports product and category destinations and exposes clear questions about seats, dashboards and general interior cleaning. The catalogue has a cleaner, scrub pads, cloths and a complete kit.
Lost because: Only 17 Search Console rows are present, with no clicks and very small impression counts. The two keyword ideas concern interior wrap and protection film, so they do not validate demand for the mapped cleaning products.
Could change if: Relevant cleaner and deep-clean-kit query data, stronger first-party impressions, or favourable sales and margin evidence could justify a product-and-kit optimisation pass.

## opportunity-microfibre_application
Credible because: The Paint Protection Cloth page already appears organically at rank six for its seed query, and the destination is technically available with substantial page content.
Lost because: The seed SERP is dominated by decorating dust sheets, masking products and paint-protection film rather than automotive buffing cloths. Search Console visibility is negligible, and this opportunity affects only one product.
Could change if: Evidence for automotive coating-removal cloth, wax-buffing cloth or sealant-removal searches could reveal a better-intent market and justify retargeting away from the ambiguous paint protection cloth phrase.

## Highest-ranked alternatives in the final recommendation set

The strategic output ranked `opportunity-car_drying`, `opportunity-contact_washing` and `opportunity-wheel_cleaning` immediately behind the winner. They remain credible alternatives because each has a complete SERP packet, mapped Search Console rows and a live destination. They lost to pre-wash because the pre-wash bundle has the clearest non-branded near-page-one traction and a product-led SERP that supports a concrete destination-consolidation intervention. Drying has more impressions but weak click capture and no relevant Keyword Ideas item in the bounded sample; contact washing has broader mixed intent and lower positions; wheel cleaning has useful long-tail visibility but a smaller query footprint. Sales, margin, stock and conversion evidence could change this ordering.

## opportunity-accessories
Credible because: The SERP is commercial and contains retailers, kits, product listings and questions about detailing accessories.
Lost because: There are no mapped affected products, no destination, no Search Console rows and no relevant returned keyword ideas. Allocating implementation effort would therefore be speculative.
Could change if: A defined accessories catalogue, a valid destination, relevant keyword demand and first-party visibility or commercial performance would be required before prioritisation.
