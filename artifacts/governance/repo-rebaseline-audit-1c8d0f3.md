## 2. Executive Verdict

The repository is a capable single-user/local prototype for product intelligence, business intelligence, evidence-bound SEO opportunity research, structured editorial planning, semantic generation, rendering, founder correction and guarded WordPress experiments. It is not the approved Product described by the current authority documents.

The strongest reusable foundations are:

* validated, hash-bound Product Intelligence, Business Intelligence and Editorial Intelligence Context records;
* provider-backed Search Console/DataForSEO/evidence contracts with provenance, freshness and cost bounds;
* opportunity, research-question, evidence, restriction and semantic-editorial validation;
* immutable artifacts and controlled one-call/zero-retry model boundaries;
* guarded WordPress read/write/recovery primitives and founder-review lineage.

The repository does not provide a customer account or connected-business onboarding surface, a customer opportunity feed/detail/evidence explorer, a complete DIY implementation route, recommendation portability/history, paid offer/charging, or a validated end-to-end paid autonomous executor. There is no tenant separation, customer permission/revocation lifecycle, production deployment verification connected to a customer flow, outcome monitoring/learning loop, competitor-gauntlet evidence, or independent validation against representative real stores. These are V1 Required under `PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`, not optional polish.

Consequently, no approved Product capability is `COMPLETE` at the full V1 customer-product level. Several bounded internal foundations are `COMPLETE` for their local contracts and proofs, while most are `KEEP` or `MODIFY`. The repository requires bounded evolution around a new product shell, data/connection boundaries and one executor contract; it should not continue the historical “AI writer” roadmap as though that were the Product.

## 4. Repository and Architecture Map

### Current layers

* **HTTP shell:** `index.js`, `app.js`, `routes/health.js`, `routes/generateArticle.js`, `routes/createSeoArticleWorkflow.js`. Express/CORS/body-parser are the only runtime dependencies.
* **Workflow contracts:** `workflows/createSeoArticle.js` defines URL-only input, immutable stage order and fail-closed stage envelopes. `createSeoArticleIntelligence.js`, `createSeoArticleM3.js`, `createSeoArticleM4B.js`, `createSeoArticleM5D.js` and `createSeoArticleM6.js` implement local article milestones. The route currently creates/executes only the earlier M2/M3-shaped boundary; there is no customer workflow API.
* **Product/business intelligence:** `product-intelligence/`, `business-intelligence/`, `editorial-intelligence/context.js`, source authority/resolution, human validation, Product Facts projection and review renderers.
* **Research:** `research/` contains DataForSEO Keyword Ideas/SERP Advanced, Search Console client/provider, evidence engine, research-state aggregation, page-level article evidence, adaptive research, claim restriction policy and validation/renderers.
* **Opportunity/interpretation:** `workflows/createSeoArticleOpportunity*.js`, `interpretation/`, controlled calls, cost calculation, prompt/brief/validation and research-state joins.
* **Editorial planning/generation:** `editorial/` semantic contracts, plans, component validation, draft provider/run/prompt/quality/render, founder revision and product-page editing; `workflows/createSeoArticleM4B.js`, `createSeoArticleM5D.js`, `createSeoArticleM6.js` bind the canonical article proofs.
* **CMS:** `cms/` WordPress authoritative reader/map, guarded writer, approvals, write plans, product map, implementation value validator and rollback simulation. It is a bounded prototype rather than a customer executor.
* **Rendering:** `rendering/`, `renderers/`, Markdown/HTML/WordPress-native renderers and media placeholder handling.
* **Historical/alternative workflows:** `cornerstone/`, `editorial/productPageWorkflow.js`, old `services/articleGeneration.js`, catalogue/providers and several WordPress plugin/deployment packages.
* **Persistence:** JSON/Markdown files under `artifacts/`; no database, queue, account store, durable customer store or tenant model was found.
* **Tests/proofs:** 84 `test/*.test.js` files plus numerous fixture/proof scripts and immutable-looking JSON/Markdown artifacts. Proofs are predominantly local, frozen or injected-provider runs.

### Actual data flow

```text
product_url
  -> deterministic PIO/BIO/EIC resolution
  -> bounded evidence/research (fixtures or configured providers)
  -> article candidate/opportunity decision
  -> M4/M4B brief + semantic page plan
  -> one controlled/injected semantic drafting call
  -> deterministic claim/lineage/render validation
  -> founder review/correction artifact
  -> optional guarded WordPress primitives (not connected to this customer flow)
```

This is a local artifact pipeline. It is not an authenticated multi-user service, a customer dashboard, a billing system or a paid executor product.
