## FAQs and buying questions

### What’s the difference between this and the XL 800GSM Drying Towel?

The Heavy Duty towel is thicker at 1200GSM and double-sided, so it holds more water and feels more substantial in the hand. The XL 800GSM towel is larger in size but lighter in feel, so it glides a bit easier. It mainly comes down to whether you prefer a lighter, larger towel or a smaller, heavier one.

### Will this towel scratch my paint?

No, not when used correctly. The plush 1200GSM fibres are very soft and are designed to glide over the surface with minimal pressure. As always, only use it on clean, shampooed paint.

### Is it too heavy when it’s wet?

It will feel heavier than a lighter towel once it’s fully loaded with water, but that’s the trade-off for the extra absorbency. The 90 × 60 cm size keeps it manageable so it doesn’t feel like you’re wrestling it.

### Can I use it on ceramic-coated or waxed cars?

Yes. It’s safe on coated, waxed and sealed paint. It won’t strip protection and works well with modern coatings.

### How many towels do I need to dry a whole car?

One Heavy Duty towel is enough for most cars and SUVs. For larger vans or if you like to swap towels halfway through, two makes the job even easier.

### How should I wash and store it?

Wash it on a cold, gentle cycle with microfibre-safe or non-bio detergent. Skip the fabric softener and air dry it flat or on a line. Store it dry, folded, and away from dirt and dust, ideally in the storage bag it came in.
